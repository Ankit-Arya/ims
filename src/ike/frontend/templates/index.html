Answer modes

Choose based on the job, not answer length.

Auto

Let the app decide

Starts with the efficient path and moves to Research when the question clearly needs broader coverage.

Example“What is BIC?” → Direct-style answer.
“Compare BIC use across all manuals.” → Research.
Direct

Fast, focused answer

One bounded search and rerank pass. Best for definitions, values, clauses and focused procedures.

Example“What is the maximum speed in RM mode?” → concise answer with the strongest citations.
Research

Broader evidence search

Expands the question, searches more broadly, synthesises across sources and verifies the answer.

Example“List all conditions and exceptions for door isolation across the manuals.”
Deep Analysis

Fuller document analysis

Asynchronous section-by-section analysis over the chosen PDFs. Use for reviews, comparisons and long structured outputs.

Example“Compare these three OTMs and identify operational differences, conflicts and risks.”
