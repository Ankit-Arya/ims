Primary generalized fixes:
- preserve bounded multi-word entities in definition/attribute questions
- extract requested facets such as definition/location/contact/constraint/enumeration
- consume planner-derived lexical/exact forms before optional query expansions
- bidirectional but conservative singular/plural bridge for PostgreSQL simple FTS
- document-diverse entity-attribute coverage lane
- protect entity coverage evidence from global reranker crowd-out
- auto-route multi-facet/entity-attribute questions to Research
- raise verification risk for multi-facet/entity coverage answers
- block corpus-wide absence claims without completed deterministic coverage
- fix fresh .env generation against the current .env.example DB placeholder

See APPLY.md for deployment commands.
